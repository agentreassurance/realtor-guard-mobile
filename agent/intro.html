How Realtor Guard Works

Realtor Guard helps real estate professionals request a verified observer for property showings and other appointments where an added safety presence is desired.

Submit your showing details and, when available, Realtor Guard will connect you with an approved observer serving your area.
Important Notice: Realtor Guard provides an additional layer of support and presence. It does not replace law enforcement, emergency services, or your own professional safety practices. Observer availability is not guaranteed. In an emergency, always call 911 or the appropriate authorities.
